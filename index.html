⚛️ Quantum Election Simulator
How It Works
🌀
Hadamard Gate
Puts each voter (qubit) into superposition — equal chance of voting either candidate. Models a completely undecided voter.
🔄
RY Rotation Gate
Applies influence as a rotation angle θ = influence × π.
Positive (+) → biases toward Candidate A
Negative (−) → biases toward Candidate B
Zero → stays 50 / 50.
🗳️
Measurement
The qubit's superposition collapses to a definite outcome — 0 (Candidate A) or 1 (Candidate B). This is the voter's final ballot.
Quantum Mapping
|0⟩ → H Undecided voter
θ > 0 Bias → Candidate A
θ = 0 Neutral (50 / 50)
θ < 0 Bias → Candidate B
Measure Final vote cast
